Developer FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Google Play closed testing compliance.

Why does Google Play require 12 testers for 14 days?

For all personal developer accounts created after November 13, 2023, Google Play requires testing your app with at least 12 or 20 opt-in testers continuously over a minimum 14-day window before applying for production release access.

How does the "Free First App" plan work?

If you are submitting your first app with 12AppTesters, select the Free First App plan (₹0). You do not need to submit any payment receipt or transaction screenshot! Simply provide your app title, package name, testing link, and contact details.

Are these real device testers?

Yes! All testers are verified human users running physical Android phones across different OS versions (Android 11 to 15). This guarantees authentic user engagement signals required by Google algorithms.

What if a tester drops off during the 14 days?

We monitor device telemetry continuous retention logs. If any device fails to check-in, our automated system instantly replaces the tester to maintain 100% continuous compliance throughout the 14 days.
