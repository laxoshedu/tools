How It Works

Step 1: Upload & Freeform Crop

Import your high-resolution image and use our freeform adjustable crop tool to slice out exactly the section you need, preserving 100% original pixel quality.

Step 2: Place & Arrange Elements

Want a collage? Click upload again! Place multiple cropped images onto your A4 canvas layout. Simply click any image to drag, scale, and rotate it perfectly into place.

Step 3: Define Your Grid Size

Set how many A4 pages wide (Columns) and tall (Rows) your final poster should be. The engine mathematically calculates the strict dimensions to prevent any stretching.

Step 4: Print & Assemble

Hit "Print Directly" for an instant 300-DPI high-quality printout, or "Download" to save the individual sliced pieces. Print them on standard A4 paper, tape them together, and hang your masterpiece!

How to Use PrintGrid

1. Upload & Crop: Click upload. Adjust the freeform box by dragging edges, and pan the image inside to crop exactly what you need.

2. Add More: You can click 'Upload Image' again to add multiple elements to your layout.

3. Adjust Grid: Set how many A4 pages wide and tall your final grid should be.

4. Transform: Click any image on the canvas to select it. Drag to move, or use sliders to scale and rotate.

5. Print/Download: Save perfectly sliced A4 pieces ready to print!
